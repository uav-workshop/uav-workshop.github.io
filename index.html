About the Workshop

Intelligent aerial networks for the next generation

The workshop focuses on next-generation UAV communication systems and the growing role of AI/ML in intelligent wireless networking. It will cover UAV networking architectures, MAC-layer protocol development, adaptive resource allocation, edge/cloud intelligence and practical system-development challenges.

The programme is intended to connect researchers, students and practitioners through expert lectures, research discussions and hands-on sessions.

About the Institution

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad is an Institute of national importance with a long tradition of excellence in teaching, research and innovation. Established in 1926, the Institute offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across science, engineering, management and interdisciplinary areas.

In 2026, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad marks its centenary year.
